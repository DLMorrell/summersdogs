Training Evaluation

$50.00

The Evaluation is required before starting any obedience or service training course. It consists of one full hour getting to know you, your dog, their baseline drive and behavior, and your specific goals.

Following the 1-hour assessment, we conduct a 1-hour introductory training lesson right then and there to immediately start addressing behavior.
