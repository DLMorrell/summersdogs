Empowering Handlers with Reliable, Life-Changing Service Dogs

Summer’s Dogs is a proud and brand new 501(c)(3) Nonprofit organization in the state of Arkansas dedicated to providing, breeding, and training task-trained service dogs for disabled individuals under the Americans with Disabilities Act.

We believe that a service dog is not only a beloved member of your family, but a critical piece of medical equipment that grants independence and improved quality of life.

Service Dogs

Task Trained Dogs to mitigate their handler’s disability

A Service Dog is for a person with a disability that hinders their life. Here at Summer’s Dogs we specialize with Autism and PTSD tasks. Service dogs undergo extensive training to perform specific tasks reliably, with calm public access and loyal focus.

Obedience Training

Training your dog while funding a future service dog!

My training methods focus on encouraging behaviors, building strong bonds, and instilling structure and respect. Your obedience lessons directly help sponsor task-trained service dogs for individuals in need. "You have a Good Pet, I want you to have a Good Dog."

Can You Be a Puppy Raiser?

Puppy Raisers are the foundation of providing fully trained Service Dogs to those in need. You take home a puppy, raise them in a loving home environment, teach socialization and basic obedience, and attend weekly training with us.

Without our volunteer raisers, our mission would not be possible!
