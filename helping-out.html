Make a Life-Changing Donation

Thank you for supporting Summer's Dogs! As a registered 501(c)(3) nonprofit organization in Arkansas, your tax-deductible donation directly funds the veterinary screenings, health genetic testing, customized gear, and high-standard task training needed to place service dogs with disabled individuals.

How your gift helps:

  • $25 feeds a future service puppy healthy, high-grade nutrition for two weeks.
  • $50 sponsors a custom Y-front training vest with patch panels.
  • $150 covers complete puppy core vaccinations and preventative care.
  • $300 funds official OFA or PennHIP joint health certifications.
  • $500+ provides comprehensive task training scholarships for low-income handlers.
