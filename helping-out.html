Volunteer Opportunity

Can You Be a Puppy Raiser?

Take home a future service puppy, provide love, structure, and socialization, and help shape a companion that will change someone's life forever.

The Commitment

Take home a puppy and raise them with love, patience, and clear guidance. Training starts the minute they arrive home with house-training rules, crate comfort, and learning how to connect with people.

Once the puppy has completed their full vaccinations, mandatory weekly in-person training begins with Summer’s Dogs. During the year the puppy is with you, you are responsible for food expenses, routine extras, and following through on weekly at-home "homework".

How Are You Helping?

Puppy Raisers are the absolute foundation of providing fully trained Service Dogs to those in need. Without our raisers, Summer’s Dogs simply couldn’t provide this service to its full extent.

You show them love, confidence, and reassurance in daily life. You teach basic manners and expose them to crowds, vehicles, noises, and real home environments—ensuring they mature into rock-solid service prospects.
