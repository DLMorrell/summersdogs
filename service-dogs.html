Self-Evaluation

Is a Service Dog Right for You?

This can be a hard question. Not only do you have to have a qualifying disability that would be mitigated by a Service Dog under the ADA, but you also have to have the correct lifestyle and resources.

1

Why do you want & need a Service Dog?

If the answer is you just want a companion or pet buddy, a Service Dog might not be the right fit for you. A service dog is medical equipment trained to perform specific life-mitigating tasks.

2

Would a Service Dog fit with your family?

If you have other pets that are untrained, reactive, or aggressive, or if your living situation prevents regular exercise and maintenance, a service dog will struggle to succeed.

3

Do you have the lifetime funds?

While Summer’s Dogs sliding scale makes receiving and training a dog accessible, you must afford food, vet care, grooming, and equipment for the dog's entire 10-14 year life.

Sliding Scale & True Cost Transparency

As a 501(c)(3) nonprofit, our mission is to ensure no disabled individual is priced out of life-saving service dog help.

Household Income Sliding Scale

Cost adjustments based on individual household need:

Summer’s Dogs is committed to ensuring that no qualified individual is priced out of life-changing service dog help. We provide an income-based sliding scale for all service dog training and placement programs.

Specific program costs and financial assistance are determined on an individual basis during the application and intake process.
