Obedience Training

Training your dog while funding a future service dog! Transform your companion with structure, clear communication, and positive behavioral encouragement.

Our Approach

"You Have a Good Pet, I Want You to Have a Good Dog."

With my training you are gaining my support and knowledge not only during lessons, but with at-home issues as well (house training as an example). My training methods focus on encouraging behaviors. This is great for dogs who have high food or toy drive and to build a strong bond and loyalty.

The dog learns to respect you. Dogs crave structure, a job, and a leader; through my training methods dogs learn all this.

I don't want to only train your dog, I want to help you train your dog. Your dog may respect me, but if they don't respect you training can be pointless. Through this form of training your dog will live a happier life, and you will have a less stressful dog.

Curriculum

The 4 Stages of Obedience

Our structured curriculum builds foundational skills into bulletproof real-world reliability.

Stage 1

The Base

Mastering foundational commands: Sit, Lay Down, Stay, and reliable Recall (Come).

Stage 2

Good Dog

Polished leash manners: Loose Leash Walking, Extended Duration Stay, Spin, and Place command.

Stage 3

Proud Parent

Proofing under distractions: Focus drills, Stay while handler is out of sight, and close Heel.

Stage 4

The Best Dog

Advanced reliability: Off-leash obedience, trick training, and AKC Canine Good Citizen (CGC) readiness.
